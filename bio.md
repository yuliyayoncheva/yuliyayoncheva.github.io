# Bio


I'm a Research Assistant Professor at [NYU Grossman School of Medicine](https://med.nyu.edu/faculty/yuliya-n-yoncheva),
in the Institute for Innovations in Medical Education and the Center for
Empathy in Medicine. My training is in cognitive neuroscience. My latest work is
on whether AI-generated feedback produces genuine learning or the appearance
of it.

I'm Principal Investigator on [Communication Compass: AI-Enhanced Feedback
System]((https://macyfoundation.org/our-grantees/ai-in-med-ed)) (Josiah Macy Jr. Foundation), one of three projects selected
nationally in AI in Medical Education. The trial pairs speech recognition with
large language models to assess how residents educate and counsel patients,
returns structured feedback drawn from their own encounters, and measures
calibration accuracy and learner agency. The system was co-designed with residents and
faculty. Co-Principal Investigator: Jesse Burk-Rafel, MD, MRes.

I'm Principal Investigator on the Empathy Project Stories Research at NYU
Langone, a twelve-month follow-up asking whether participants can still name,
unprompted, the practices they picked up for exercising empathy.

Prior to this, I directed the educational intervention design and implementation for an
NIH-funded randomized clinical trial examining brain plasticity in children learning new skills. 

I co-founded the POD Network's [AI in Education Special Interest Group](https://podnetwork.org/governance/sigs/) (founded in 2023, 3,000+ members) and moderated the research session at the [Teaching & Learning with Generative AI Virtual Symposium](https://wp.nyu.edu/2025notebooklm/). I also facilitate a grassroots peer community for [decision science](mailto:admin@mbd-alumni.com) practitioners. My conference work arc traces early questions of AI literacy and adoption ([2023](https://wp.nyu.edu/2023aiconference/schedule)) to the harder problems of redesign and learner agency ([2025](https://wp.nyu.edu/ltt2025/detailed-schedule/); [2026](https://wp.nyu.edu/ltt2026/talk-descriptions/)).

I write on [Substack](https://open.substack.com/pub/yuliyayoncheva/p/escaping-the-ai-convenience-trap?r=22jisy) about metacognition, decision making, and the future
of human learning.
